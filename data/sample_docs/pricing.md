# Flowstack Pricing

Flowstack offers three plans designed to scale with your team: Starter, Growth, and Enterprise. All prices are in USD. You can switch plans at any time; changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

## Plans & Pricing

**Starter — Free.** For small teams getting started. Includes up to 5 users, 10 active workflows, 1,000 workflow runs per month, and community support. No credit card required.

**Growth — $15 per user per month, billed annually ($180 per user per year).** If billed monthly, Growth is $18 per user per month. Includes unlimited workflows, 50,000 workflow runs per month, version history, SSO/SAML, audit logs, and priority email support. Most teams of 10 to 200 people choose Growth.

**Enterprise — Custom pricing.** For organizations with advanced security, compliance, or scale needs. Includes everything in Growth plus dedicated infrastructure, custom data retention, SCIM provisioning, a named customer success manager, 99.99% uptime SLA, and onboarding assistance. Contact sales@flowstack.example for a quote.

## Billing FAQ

**How am I billed?** Subscriptions are billed per active user ("seat"). A user counts as active if they log in or trigger a workflow run during the billing month. Deactivated users are not billed.

**What payment methods do you accept?** We accept all major credit cards and ACH bank transfer on annual plans. Enterprise customers can pay by invoice with net-30 terms.

**Can I change plans mid-cycle?** Yes. Upgrades apply immediately with prorated charges. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date.

**Do you offer refunds?** Annual plans can be refunded in full within the first 30 days. After 30 days, refunds are prorated for unused full months. Monthly plans are non-refundable but can be cancelled at any time.

**Is there a discount for nonprofits or education?** Yes — registered nonprofits and accredited educational institutions receive 50% off Growth and Enterprise plans. Apply from the billing page with proof of status.
